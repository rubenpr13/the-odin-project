Rabo de Toro

Traditional Spanish dish made with the tail of a bull, slow-cooked in a rich sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 bull's tail (about 500g)
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 can of tomatoes (400g)
  • 1 liter of beef or chicken broth
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  • Cut the bull's tail into pieces and season with salt and pepper.
  • In a large pot, sauté the onions and garlic until translucent.
  • Add the bull's tail and cook for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  • Add the red bell pepper and cook for another 5 minutes.
  • Pour in the tomatoes and broth, add the bay leaves and oregano.
  • Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for about 2 hours, or until the meat is tender.
  • Remove the bay leaves and adjust seasoning if needed.
  • Serve hot with rice or bread.
