Salmorejo

A traditional Spanish cold soup made with tomatoes, bread, garlic, and olive oil.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg ripe red tomatoes
  • 200 g day-old Cordoban telera bread (or any dense white bread)
  • 150 ml extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 garlic clove (germ removed)
  • 5–10 g salt
  • To serve: chopped hard-boiled egg, diced Iberian ham and a drizzle of olive oil

Steps

  1. Wash the tomatoes, cut them into pieces and blend them.
  2. Strain the tomato through a sieve or food mill to remove the skins and seeds.
  3. Tear the bread into pieces and soak it in the tomato for about 10 minutes, until soft.
  4. Add the garlic and salt and blend until smooth.
  5. Slowly add the olive oil while blending, so it emulsifies into a thick, creamy texture.
  6. Taste and adjust the salt.
  7. Chill in the fridge for at least 1 hour.
  8. Serve cold, topped with the egg, the ham and a drizzle of olive oil.
