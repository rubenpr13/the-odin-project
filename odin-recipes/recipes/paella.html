Paella

The most popular and delicious Spanish dish.

Ingredients

  • 400 g short-grain rice (bomba or senia)
  • 500 g chicken, cut into pieces
  • 500 g rabbit, cut into pieces
  • 200 g flat green beans (ferraura)
  • 100–150 g garrofó (large white Valencian lima beans)
  • 1 ripe tomato, grated
  • 100 ml olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika
  • A few saffron threads
  • 1.2–1.5 l water
  • Salt
  • Optional: snails (vaquetes) and a sprig of rosemary

Steps

  1. Heat the oil over medium heat in a level paella pan.
  2. Slowly brown the chicken and rabbit with a little salt for 15–20 minutes.
  3. Add the green beans and garrofó and fry for 5 minutes.
  4. Make a space in the centre and fry the grated tomato.
  5. Add the paprika, stir for a few seconds without letting it burn, then pour in the water.
  6. Add the saffron and simmer for 20–30 minutes. Adjust the salt: it should taste slightly salty.
  7. Spread the rice evenly across the pan. Do not stir from this point on.
  8. Cook for 8–10 minutes over high heat, then 8–10 minutes over medium-low heat.
  9. For the socarrat (the crispy bottom layer), turn up the heat for 1–2 minutes at the end.
  10. Cover with a clean cloth and let it rest for 5 minutes before serving.
