Croquetas

Traditional Spanish croquettes made with Serrano ham, béchamel sauce, and breadcrumbs.

Ingredients

  • 1 l whole milk
  • 100 g plain flour
  • 50 g butter
  • 50 ml olive oil
  • 150 g Serrano or Iberian ham, very finely chopped
  • ½ small onion, very finely chopped (optional)
  • Nutmeg
  • Salt (just a little, as the ham is already salty)
  • For coating: 2 beaten eggs and breadcrumbs
  • Olive oil for frying

Steps

  • Warm the milk and set it aside.
  • Melt the butter with the oil over medium heat. If using onion, cook it gently for 5 minutes.
  • Add the ham and cook for 1 minute.
  • Add the flour and cook for 2–3 minutes, stirring, so it doesn't taste raw.
  • Pour in the warm milk a little at a time, stirring constantly, until there are no lumps.
  • Add the nutmeg, and taste before adding any salt.
  • Cook over medium-low heat for 15–20 minutes, stirring, until the dough pulls away from the sides of the pan.
  • Transfer the dough to a dish, cover it with cling film touching the surface, and chill in the fridge for at least 4 hours, ideally overnight.
  • Shape the croquettes, then dip them in the egg and coat them in breadcrumbs.
  • Fry in small batches in plenty of very hot oil (about 180 °C) until golden.
  • Drain on kitchen paper and serve hot.
